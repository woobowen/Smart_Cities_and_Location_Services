# E-B-001 · Reinforcement learning method and metric contract

Source: advanced student notebook cells 26–43, read directly from teacher-provided 归档.zip. Execution: `/root/reinforcement`, native child context. No test data have been inspected to select policy parameters.

- Teaching model: NYC bounding box [40.60,40.88] × [-74.05,-73.75], x=(lon+74.05)·111cos40.74°, y=(lat−40.60)·111 km. source_crs=UNVERIFIED; these are conditional working coordinates, not geographic accuracy claims.
- Manhattan km, 25 km/h, 10 min steps, [06:00,24:00), T=108. Orders proxy adjacent check-ins, not ride-hailing ground truth. 1,500 resampled proxy orders per simulated day; 300 actual training episodes are 300 simulations.
- 8×8 zones; pickup Manhattan distance ≤3 km; unique drivers/orders at each dispatch, drivers unavailable during ceil((pickup+trip)/4.1667) steps. Positions become available at destination only on release. Fare proxy=5+2·trip_km (teaching currency, not actual fares).
- Patience=2 allows age 0,1,2 steps and cancels at age>2. Cancellation count, horizon-unassigned, accepted, completed-before-T, and outstanding rides are separately recorded. Teaching revenue is accepted fare at dispatch, including rides finishing after T; completed-before-T revenue is separately available.
- Q action=destination-zone or WAIT. Each transition records actual feasible destination zones at next free time before centralized matching. TD target r+γ^delta max(Q[next, actual avail∪WAIT]); terminal next time≥T has target r. α=max(1/N,.05); V(next) is sample mean of each observed feasible max; V(T)=0.
- LT(t,i,k)=γ^DELTA(i,k)V(min(t+DELTA,T),k)−γV(t+1,i). DELTA uses region centers, whereas simulation duration uses actual pickup+trip. β=.5 teaching reference. Nonpositive/infeasible edge weights excluded. β=0 is exactly KM-fare.
- Double Q: randomly update one table using its argmax over actual avail∪WAIT, evaluate that action in other table. V samples mean of the two cross-evaluated greedy values. Each table has its own visit counts. Bias reduction is a hypothesis, not assumed outcome.
- Repositioning: only unmatched idle drivers; select 4-neighbor zone center whose discounted arrival V exceeds waiting V. Manhattan distance, ceil travel time, unavailable while traveling, position updated on release; empty distance/time tracked. No instantaneous motion or free cost. Gross teaching revenue, deadhead distance, and illustrative net revenue (predeclared 1 currency/km, diagnostic only) separately reported.
- Data: first 70% observed dates train+validation, final30% held-out test. Last20% of training dates used as validation for parameter/candidate choice. Initial morning driver locations strictly drawn from training partition; no all-checkin global variable. All final test seeds fixed before evaluation.
- Evaluation: paired simulated days, common initial conditions/order stream, accepted revenue/response/mean pickup/cancel/end states/reposition cost/nonpositive-edge rejection/real compute time. Empty denominator→NaN, never fake zero. Validation selection maximizes mean accepted revenue with response no worse than teaching QKM−0.01; otherwise retain teaching baseline. Final test is confirmation, no retuning.
- Initial budget: hand calculations and counterexamples; 3-episode pilot; teacher fleet40/60/100×300episodes; β validation[0,.2,.4,.5,.6,.8,1]; γ[.8,.92,.95], GRID[6,8,10], patience[1,2,3] single factors; Double and reposition components then supported combinations. Five paired simulation repeats and train seeds when teacher/source requires or mechanism uncertainty warrants. Later follow-up experiments require observed-result rationale; finite budget, no guaranteed gains.
- QKM+NSGAII bonus: first verified standalone components, then two-objective feasible assignment front (maximize QKM edge value, minimize pickup). Estimated long-term value is never reported as realized income. Knee and constraint choices fixed on validation. Runtime/n_eval retained.
