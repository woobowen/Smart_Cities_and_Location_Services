usage: execute_fresh_notebooks.py [-h] [--execute] --output OUTPUT
                                  [--jobs {1,2}]
                                  [--expected-current-sha256 EXPECTED_CURRENT_SHA256]
                                  [--expected-figure-generator-sha256 EXPECTED_FIGURE_GENERATOR_SHA256]

Execute existing Goal 2 notebooks in at most two isolated OS workers.
Preparation alone never launches a kernel. --execute and frozen input hashes
are required; the existing generator/executor remain the only notebook
writers.

  --execute
  --output OUTPUT
  --jobs {1,2}
  --expected-current-sha256 EXPECTED_CURRENT_SHA256
